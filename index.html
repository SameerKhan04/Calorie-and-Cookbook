Privacy policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

Calorie & Cookbook is a food log and meal planner for Android and the web, aimed at Australia. This policy says what the app collects, why, and who else receives it. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

You give us most of this when you create an account and use the app.

Information Examples
Account Email address and password, or a Google account if you choose Google sign-in. We never see your Google password. A sign-in token stays on your device until you sign out.
Profile Display name, sex, age, height, weight, activity, goal, meals per day, units, diet tags, kitchen equipment, preferred shops, and an optional weekly food budget in Australian dollars.
Food log Foods you save or bookmark, barcodes, diary entries (including time, meal, quantity, and notes), recipes, meal plans, pantry items, and the shopping list derived from your plan.
Weight Weigh-ins you choose to record, used to suggest a calorie-target change. A suggestion is never applied unless you tap to apply it.
Photos and descriptions A meal photo or a typed description, only when you ask for a nutrition estimate. See below.
Notifications If you turn on the remaining-meal reminder, a push token and whether the device is Android, iOS, or web. The reminder is optional and at most once a day.

The camera is used to scan a barcode or photograph a meal. We do not record video, and we do not read your photo library.

We do not sell personal information. The app does not show ads and does not include a third-party analytics or advertising SDK.

How we use it

  • Create and keep your account signed in.
  • Calculate calorie and macro targets from the profile you enter, and show what you have logged against them.
  • Save recipes, pantry items, meal plans, and a shopping list.
  • Look up packaged foods when you search or scan a barcode.
  • Suggest recipes from your pantry, diet tags, and budget.
  • Send the optional remaining-meal reminder.

Targets and photo estimates are calculations for food logging. They are not medical advice, a diagnosis, or a personal diet prescribed for you.

Meal photos and estimates

A photo estimate sends the image, and any note you type, to our server. We use it to produce a nutrition estimate and we do not save the image in your account. The same is true of a typed meal description: we return an estimate, and we store a diary entry only if you confirm it.

If an AI key is configured, that request goes to the first provider that can answer, in this order: Google Gemini, Groq, NVIDIA, then OpenAI. NVIDIA receives text only. Gemini, Groq, and OpenAI may receive the photo. If no key is configured, the estimate is calculated on our server and the photo is not sent to those companies.

Recipe ideas can send your pantry item names, diet tags, and budget figure to the same providers. They do not send your name, email, sex, height, or weight.

We do not use your diary to train a model of our own. A provider that receives a photo or description handles it under its own privacy policy. We do not have a separate promise from those providers that they delete it immediately.

Who else receives it

We share information only to run the features above.

Recipient What they receive
Supabase Hosts accounts and the database: profile, foods, diary, recipes, meal plan, pantry, weight check-ins, and push tokens. Row access is limited to your own user id.
Google If you choose Google sign-in, Google authenticates you and Supabase receives the resulting account identity (such as your email).
Open Food Facts The search words or barcode you look up. We do not send your account id with that request. Open Food Facts is a public food database at world.openfoodfacts.org.
Google Gemini, Groq, NVIDIA, OpenAI Only when that provider is configured and you use an estimate or recipe ideas, as described above.
Expo Your push token and the text of a reminder, so Expo can deliver it through the phone’s notification service. Only if you turn reminders on.

These services may process information outside Australia, including in the United States. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Where it is kept

Account data is stored in Supabase until you delete the account or ask us to delete it. Signing out removes the session from your device. It does not delete the account.

An unfinished food log can sit in a queue on the device until it syncs. That queue is on your phone, not in an advertising profile.

We do not keep meal photos. After an account is deleted, the live database rows for that user are removed. A host backup, if one exists, drops off on that host’s normal cycle.

Your choices

  • You can edit your display name, diet tags, budget, and macro targets in You.
  • Push reminders are off until you turn them on, and you can turn them off again.
  • You can skip photo estimates and log food by search, barcode, or a custom food.
  • You can sign out at any time. Ask us if you want the account deleted. There is no delete button in the app yet.
  • You can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold, or for a correction if it is wrong.

Children

The app is meant for adults. Onboarding asks for age so calorie maths has an input. We do not aim the product at children, and we do not use anyone’s information for advertising. If you believe we have an account for a child, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.

Contact

To ask for a copy of your information, a correction, or deletion of your account, use the contact address in the store listing for Calorie & Cookbook.
